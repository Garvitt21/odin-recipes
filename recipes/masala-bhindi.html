Description:

Bhindi Coated with a light, spicy masala and pan-friend until crisp on the outside while remaining tender inside. It's simple, flavorful, and doesn't required a seperate gravy

Incredients - 2 servings

  • 250g bhindi (okra)
  • 1 small onion. thinly sliced
  • 1-2 green chillies, chopped
  • 1½ tbsp oil
  • ½ tsp cumin seeds
  • ½turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • ½ tsp red chilli powder
  • ½ tsp amchur powder
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • Salt, to taste
  • 1 tbsp besan (optional , for extra crispiness)

Steps:

  1. Prepare the bhindi

    Wash the bhindi and dry it completely with a towel. Cut off the ends and slice into 1-2 cm pieces.

  2. Cook the Bhindi

    Heat oil in a wide pan. Add cumin seeds and let them crackle. Add the bhindi and cook on medium-high heat , uncovered, for about 7-10 minutes. Stir occasionally.

  3. Add onion and chilli

    Add sliced onion and green chilli. Cook for another 3-4 minutes until onion starts becoming golden.

  4. Add spices

    Lower the heat and add turmeric , coriander powder , chilli powder and salt. Mix gently so you don't mash the bhindi.

  5. Make it crispy

    If using besan, sprinle 1 tbsp besan over the bhindi and mix. Cook for uncovereed for another 3-5 minutes untill the besan becomes slightly toasted and bhindi looks crisp.

  6. Finish

    Turn off the heat and add amchur and garam masala. Toss once and server hot.

Important: Don't cover the pan while cooking bhindi. Trapped steam makes it slimy than crispy.
